The Drift Meter

A Claude-assisted learning exercise for responsible AI adoption in judgment-intensive nonprofit work.

The Drift Meter is a lightweight prototype for helping mission-driven teams notice how AI changes their decision process. It runs you through a charity cost-effectiveness review twice, once with Claude as a partner and once on your own, and records not your decisions but the texture of your judgment: how many assumptions you interrogate, how much you read, when you accept the framing you are handed, and how you sit with uncertainty.

Most people feel sharper with the assistant while leaning on it more than they notice. Seeing exactly where that happens is how a team adopts Claude in ways that build internal capacity rather than quiet dependency. At the end, it shows you the two sides of your judgment side by side, and at cohort scale as an Agential Health Index.
